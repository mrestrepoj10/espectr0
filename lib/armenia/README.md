# Armenia seismic-microzonation research dossier

Status: **historical applicability confirmed; numerical annex still missing**.

CRQ Resolution 075 of 2006 expressly states that Decreto Municipal 079 of 29 September 2000 is applicable in Armenia. The official POT Volume 4-B independently records Decreto 079 as the instrument adopting the seismic zoning after the 1999 earthquake. Those official findings govern the historical model; CAP review and adoption of the separately delivered 2025 update are currentness context, not blockers to recognizing Decreto 079.

The localized data gap is narrower: the official bytes for Decreto 079 and its complete technical annex were not recovered. Consequently, the exact zones, hazard cases, coefficients, Am/An or equivalent parameters, piecewise equations, branch bounds and site-specific rules cannot yet be transcribed into a calculator. EspectroCol may be used to compare option naming and UX, but never to fill these missing numbers.

The proposed spectrum matrix remains exactly empty. No engine, UI, map, GIS lookup or automatic zone selection is added by this dossier. A professional must apply the controlling official instruments, including the project-specific geotechnical requirements identified by the POT.

Official sources remain external-only and byte-locked where recovered. The negative oracle rejects only unsupported numerical requests; it does not characterize the historical instrument as unadopted or inapplicable.
