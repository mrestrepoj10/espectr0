# Armenia applicability analysis

The official evidence supports two separate source lineages:

1. **Historical model:** CRQ Resolution 075 of 2006 says Decreto Municipal 079 of 29 September 2000 is applicable in Armenia. The official POT Volume 4-B also identifies Decreto 079 as the act adopting the seismic zoning.
2. **Later update:** the December 2025 delivery notice records that the new study still required Commission approval and later municipal officialization at that date.

The second fact does not suspend or erase the first. The dossier therefore no longer treats CAP approval, a new municipal decree, NSR-10 harmonization, or an independent-review formality as blockers to recognizing the historical source.

Calculator output remains unavailable for one concrete reason: the primary Decreto 079 bytes and complete numerical annex—zone matrix, hazards, coefficients, equations, bounds and site-specific branches—have not been recovered from an official repository. That missing numerical package is the localized activation gap.
