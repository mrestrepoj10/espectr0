# Adoption and supersession analysis

## Result

No Armenia municipal spectrum is ready for activation. The evidence supports a typed **blocked** decision, not a choice between historical coefficients.

## Legal and technical chronology

1. The 2009 POT technical support identifies Decreto Municipal 079 de 2000 as the instrument that adopted Armenia's seismic zoning and replaced NSR-98 sections A.2.4 and A.2.6 for the mapped zones. That citation establishes the historical legal lineage, but the official decree bytes and complete adopted technical annex were not available in the official repositories inspected.
2. Acuerdo 019 de 2009 requires rigorous use of the municipal microzonation criteria together with the sismo-resistant standard then in force. It does not itself reproduce the exact zone × hazard × coefficient matrix or harmonize the 2000 instrument with NSR-10.
3. In Acta 153 of 7 February 2019, the Comisión Asesora Permanente answered a request from Armenia's planning department. It stated that prior studies must be updated and harmonized with the current NSR-10 under A.2.9.5. This prevents treating the NSR-98-era model as automatically current.
4. The Government of Quindío reported that a new study was delivered to Armenia on 10 December 2025. The same official notice says the municipality still had to obtain Commission approval and then officialize the study by decree.
5. The official municipal decree index snapshot retrieved 25 July 2026 did not contain `microzonificación`, `microzonificacion`, or `Decreto 079`. This is corroborating, not conclusive: the index may be incomplete. No adoption claim rests on absence alone.

## Reference-site reconciliation

Any A/B/C/D or other Armenia model exposed by a third-party calculator is only a UX observation. It cannot establish the current zone model, hazards, return periods, damping, coefficients, equations, warnings, site-specific branches, adoption, or building compatibility. No value from such a site, Scribd mirror, thesis, or article is canonicalized here.

## Activation requirements

Activation requires all of the following primary evidence:

- the complete official technical package and exact edition;
- the Commission approval or equivalent legally required review;
- the municipal adoption decree and effective date;
- amendments, errata and supersession analysis;
- exact selectable zones and manual professional-identification rules;
- exact hazards, probabilities/return periods, damping and units;
- exact direct coefficients, formulas, branch domains and site-specific cases;
- field-level source regions and an independent numerical oracle.

Until then, the product must retain the NSR-10 national workflow for Armenia and expose no municipal zone selector, calculation, export or building-workflow capability.
