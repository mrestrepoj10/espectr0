# Adoption and currentness analysis

## Adopted instrument

The governing municipal instrument is Decreto 411.0.20.0158 of 18 March 2014,
issued by the Alcaldía de Santiago de Cali. Article 1 adopts the seismic
microzonation; Article 4 adopts the response curves and coefficient tables;
Article 11 identifies the 2005 INGEOMINAS–DAGMA study as technical support; and
Article 12 makes the decree effective from issuance and publication. The final
page bears the Official Bulletin 038 stamp dated 19 March 2014.

The official municipal scan is locked as `cali-decree-0158-2014`: 20 physical
pages, 7,754,229 bytes, SHA-256
`8c58d68e34b1f79b5227beb98b58ca6fc8fb24031cb8d2c64ebecf71873ce9d6`.

## Subsequent municipal confirmation

Article 55 of Acuerdo 0373 of 2014 (the adopted POT revision) directs the
municipal administration to promote and oversee application of Decreto 0158 and
to arrange an update when new soil-behavior information becomes available. This
is affirmative evidence of continued applicability later in 2014, not a new set
of coefficients.

Targeted searches of official Cali publications and normative repositories for
acts that modify, replace, or repeal Decreto 0158 found none as of 25 July 2026.
The dossier therefore records the decree as active, with a mandatory currentness
recheck before any future product activation because official repositories are
dynamic.

## Technical lineage and non-substitution

The 2005 INGEOMINAS–DAGMA technical study is historical support, not the final
harmonized rule. Its damage-threshold assumptions (`Ad=0.05`, ten-year return)
differ from Decreto 0158 (`Ad=0.09`) and the NSR-10 return-period definition
(approximately 31 years). The adopted decree and NSR-10 definition govern the
research dossier; historical values are retained only in the conflict ledger.

This adoption conclusion does not cure the separate computational gap in the
decree: Table 4 omits `A0d` and `Fa`, which its damage-threshold graph requires
for `T≤Tcd`. Activation remains blocked on that primary-source ambiguity and
pending independent review.
